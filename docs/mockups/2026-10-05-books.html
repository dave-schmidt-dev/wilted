Books and source-kind grouping

Q033 (books as a source kind) and Q034 (group by source kind). Static mockup, synthetic data. Nothing here is built; David selects an option or defers the area.

Q034 is conditional on Q033: a third kind is what makes one undifferentiated list untenable. Selecting Q033 implies choosing one of these groupings.

Option A: Kind sections inside the Menu

The Menu groups by source kind: Podcasts, Articles, Books. A book is one row with progress; opening it shows ordered parts.

Data contract
  • Work: id (namespaced per W-INV-003), kind audio|print, title, author, parts [Part].
  • Part: id, order, duration or text length. Position: (partID, offset), synced across devices.
  • Ingestion: local audio files, EPUB and PDF. DRM-protected files are refused with a reason. Transcription and print-to-audio chunk and resume after interruption.
  • The grouping is presentation only; storage already namespaces by kind.
