MOCKUP — NOT THE SHIPPING APP
WILTED

Proposed sidebar. Three items where there are five today. Larder is gone because it was the Menu under another name, and Prep is gone because preparing is a state an episode is in, not a place it goes.

Today: Larder, Feeds, Prep, Menu, Settings.

Menu

The Larder and the Menu are the same thing: a place for episodes to wait. So there is one waiting place. Prep is not a third place either — an episode being prepared is an episode waiting, so preparation is a state, not a destination that owns its own buttons. That leaves Feeds, what arrived and is not yours yet, and Menu, everything waiting for you.

Feeds asks one question: keep this or skip it. That is the whole surface. Every other decision — download, process, play — is about an episode you already kept, so it belongs on the Menu and appears nowhere else.

The Menu reads in one fixed order: Ready first, because that is what you can play right now; Downloaded next, one step away; Available last, two steps away. Filters at the top jump to a single group, and every group carries its own bulk action — so showing or acting on a whole group is one click, and the two you reach for most, Download all new and Prepare all downloaded, sit in the toolbar where the filter does not hide them.

PROPOSED — two destinations

Feeds is the inbox. Episodes arrive here from subscriptions. Nothing here is yours yet; the only decision is whether you want it.

Menu is where episodes wait, in order. Saving and queueing were never two things — an episode you keep is an episode you intend to hear. Preparing, ready, and not-yet-downloaded are filters across that one list, because they are states of waiting, not separate rooms.

One decision per surface. Feeds offers Keep and Skip, nothing else. The Menu offers the single step a row is waiting for — Download, then Prepare, then Play — plus a way to take it off the list. A row never shows three greyed-out buttons, because its group already says which step it is on.

Skip becomes Completed once you have started an episode. Removing something you never began is skipping it; removing something you are part-way through is finishing with it. Same position, honest word, and neither deletes anything.

TODAY — four destinations

Feeds carries 15 buttons — Download, Prepare, Stop preparation, Retry, Play now, Add to Menu, Skip, Remove, Unsubscribe and more — so the inbox asks for every decision at once about episodes you have not even chosen to keep. Larder has 3, Prep 7, Menu 3. Play now is on three surfaces; Add to Menu, Prepare, Stop preparation and Retry are on two each.

Larder and Menu already overlap and neither name says which one an episode waits in, so the same episode is listed twice with different actions and no statement of which list is authoritative.

Skip on Feeds deletes the episode's records; Remove on Menu takes it out of the queue. Two words, two behaviours, nothing on screen distinguishing them. Neither says anything about whether you had started listening.
