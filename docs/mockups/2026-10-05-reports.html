Headlines, weather and morning report

Q013 (headlines and weather interruptions) and Q014 (morning report). Static mockup, synthetic data. Nothing here is built; David selects an option or defers the area.

The constraint from the owner: podcast listening position and completion state survive any interruption.

Option A: Reports play only at boundaries

Headlines and weather never cut into an episode. They play between items, and the morning report is one generated item at the top of the Menu.

Data contract
  • ReportSource: kind headlines|weather, source, staleAfter; a stale or unreachable source is skipped with a notice, never spoken stale.
  • MorningReport: generated item composed of selected sources; trigger is the first launch after a set time, or manual.
  • Episode position and completion are never written by a report. An episode finishing normally is the only boundary.

Option B: Reports may interrupt at pause points

Headlines and weather can interrupt an episode at the next pause (silence or chapter). A resume chip returns to the exact position.
