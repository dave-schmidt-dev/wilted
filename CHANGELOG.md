# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- `make native-mac-unit TEST=<Class[/testMethod],...> ITERATIONS=<n>` and `make native-ios-unit` run one Mac or iOS unit test class or method up to 100 times through the native gate (`WILTED_MAC_UNIT_SELECTORS`/`WILTED_MAC_UNIT_ITERATIONS` and the `WILTED_IOS_UNIT_*` pair); malformed values fail before any leg starts, a selected Mac run's test-count floor drops to 1, and the run reports as filtered, so it never mints a receipt.
- The Mac app keeps its own warnings, errors and faults in `~/Library/Application Support/Wilted/logs/wilted.log` (at most 1 MiB, with three older files), so a problem can still be read after the system log has dropped it. Only values the app already logs as public (such as episode IDs, feed hosts and error descriptions) are written; everything else appears as `<private>`.
- Every podcast preparation run records which Wilted build (version, build number and source commit), which ad engine version and which ad-removal code revision processed it, and the Mac episode info popover shows it (with the build and reason when the newest attempt failed), so a missed ad or an error can be checked against the fix that addressed it. `make install` stamps the app with the commit it was built from and refuses a build that does not carry it.
- The iPhone Larder shows read-only Mac preparation reports before audio is available, with stage-local progress and separate associated-copy status; media filters and playback admission retain their existing meaning.
- The Mac publishes current preparation progress for kept episodes through the existing debounced library sync, with exact retry identity, curated stage/count text and automatic success/withdrawal clearing. Older attempts cannot replace current status or invalidate a newer Ready revision.
- Library entries carry an optional, versioned, display-only preparation status (attempt ID, state, stage, detail, fraction, update time, optional revision) bounded to 128/64/240 UTF-8 bytes; a missing, unsupported or malformed value is ignored so the entry remains fully usable, and the field never changes media admission, playback or phone authority.
- A Larder row's context menu offers Review Possible Duplicate for podcast copies: an exact same-show pair is shown with read-only evidence (raw episode IDs and publisher GUIDs, original-download and prepared-revision proof, progress, decisions, queue slots and ambiguity hints), nothing changes until you compare the pair, confirm it, and review the prepared consequences before Apply. Changing the pair or representative voids the old comparison and confirmation. Stale previews are refused, and reversal is equally explicit, naming each member that loses inherited Keep or suppression before later feed automation can retire it and delete unprotected media.
- The feed settings popover explains inline when Auto prepare On is suppressed by global Processing Manual while Prepare everything downloaded is Off.
- Reports (off by default): spoken news headlines and National Weather Service alerts interrupt playback at the next pause in speech, then resume where you were, on the Mac and the iPhone; each device uses its own approximate location, never your IP address. The Mac can build a morning report with the forecast and headlines, and Settings shows its status with Build now.
- Article feeds: follow a feed without audio, refresh it for new posts, and add the ones you want from the Feeds inbox; a News switch in the feed popover marks news feeds.
- Audiobooks and books on the Mac: Import book in the Add sheet (or drop files) imports audio files or a folder as one audiobook with chapters, and turns EPUB or PDF text into a spoken book; progress shows with Cancel, and refused files say why. Importing the same files again finds the same book.
- Chapters: book rows show their chapter, and the Mac player and iPhone player step between chapters.
- Playlists on the Mac: a Playlists section in the sidebar holds manual playlists (Add to Playlist from a Larder row, reorder, remove) and automatic playlists built from rules. Playing a playlist never changes the Larder order.
- iPhone Add (Larder toolbar): search Apple Podcasts or paste a link, and the phone asks your Mac to subscribe or add the article, showing "Sent to your Mac" until the Mac answers; an older Mac shows "Update Wilted on your Mac to add from iPhone" and nothing is sent.
- One Add sheet on the Mac (toolbar Add or ⌘N) replaces the Feeds Subscribe popover and the Larder article field: paste a podcast or article link, or search Apple Podcasts by name, and shows you already follow are marked.
- The Mac answers subscribe and add-article requests sent from a phone, and rejects requests it cannot read instead of silently dropping them.
- An Apple Watch remote for the iPhone (Development builds): Now Playing with play, pause, skip, speed and sleep, and Up Next; it always shows the phone's last update and its age, and disables its controls with a note while the phone is out of reach.
- Per-feed automation on the Mac: each feed in Feeds has a settings popover for Auto keep, Auto download, Auto prepare (On, Off or Use global) and a Kept limit, with resolved values shown inline beside Use global. Settings holds the global defaults (Auto keep Off, download Off, prepare Off, no limit), so nothing changes until you turn something on.
- A full feed makes new episodes wait for space instead of removing anything: an existing, playing or part-heard episode is never removed to make room, and finishing, completing or removing a kept episode lets the oldest waiting one in.
- Per-feed match rules: ordered patterns on title or notes that Keep or Skip new episodes, with inline errors, a preview of every episode's verdict, and "Apply to existing", which changes only undecided and automatically decided episodes, never your own choices or anything you have started, and can be undone.
- Your own Keep, Skip and Restore, on the Mac or the iPhone, are recorded as yours, and automation never reverses them.
- Mac automatic-playlist rule editor: stable numbered rules combine with case-insensitive AND, OR and NOT and parentheses (for example `(1 OR 2 OR 3 OR 4 OR 5) AND 6 AND 7` over feed, Unplayed and Ready leaves), preview the matching count and titles live, and explain an invalid formula inline.
- The Mac sidebar can be hidden and shown from a toolbar button or Control-Command-S, and the choice is remembered; a hidden sidebar gives its room to the side player.
- The Mac reopens where you left it: the Feeds selection and Off the list disclosure, each page's scroll position, the Larder filter and search, and unsent drafts are restored at launch. View > Clear Saved View resets them.
- Share in the Mac players and the iPhone episode detail shares the episode's own web page when the feed gives one; otherwise it shares the title and show and says "No episode page", never the feed address.
- The iPhone Larder has a Sort menu (Play order, Newest, Oldest, Shortest, Title) and a Group menu (None, Feed). They only change how the list is shown and are remembered on the phone; while a sort other than Play order is active the list says playback and Next still follow the play order, which is unchanged for auto-continue, CarPlay and Siri.
- The Mac records lifetime listening totals in its library: minutes played, audio downloaded, and minutes manually skipped (forward seeks and Next), alongside the existing four totals.
- Mac Settings shows seven lifetime totals (the existing four plus minutes played, GB downloaded and minutes manually skipped) with the date tracking started. While the totals load or rebuild after the upgrade, Settings says so; if they cannot be read, Settings offers a retry and the Larder still opens normally.
- Mac Settings shows library sync status: changes waiting to send, when this Mac last sent and read changes, errors, rate limits and account problems, with one Sync now button and an account review step. It describes this Mac's own sends; whether the phone has fetched them is shown on the phone.
- The Now Playing position bar is a real scrubber, on the full player and the mini player's line: drag or tap to pick a time, the chosen time shows live, playback moves once on release, and VoiceOver adjusts it by the skip lengths.
- Mark completed and Remove from Larder on Now Playing and the episode detail, each asking first; the Larder rows now use swipes (not downloaded: right removes, left downloads; downloaded: right marks the episode completed, started or not, left plays), a full swipe only opens the question, and VoiceOver gets each action by name. The inline completion tick is gone.
- The iPhone shows only what the Mac reports ready (Larder, CarPlay, Siri, play order); an episode the Mac says it has no ready audio for leaves the list at once and returns when the Mac offers it.
- A soft wash of the playing episode's artwork across the top of Now Playing, from the local cache only, with text contrast pinned in a test.
- Transcripts show who is speaking when the Mac's transcript names speakers (the speaker is optional on the wire, so an older Mac or phone is unaffected).
- Hosted headless UI tests for the Larder list, episode detail, Settings, transcript and Now Playing (touch-target sizes, VoiceOver actions, swipe states and their confirmations).
- W-INV-016: the Mac, iPhone, CarPlay and Siri surfaces keep one Wilted look, design and behavior (shared symbols, watermark, palette, episode rows and play order); platform differences are limited to the Mac's rich sorting and two-pane layout, the iPhone's simplicity, CarPlay's templates and Siri being voice-only.
- A Siri Intents extension (`WiltediOSIntents`, `com.zerodelta.wilted.ios.intents`) confirms spoken "play ..." requests ("Hey Siri", the car's Siri button) and hands them to the app, which plays the downloaded episode. Release ships without it until Siri does.
- CarPlay now opens on a tab bar: Downloaded (the episode list) and Shows (one row per show with downloaded episodes, its artwork and "N downloaded"; a tap opens that show's episodes). Episode rows show the time left (or the full length marked New), a listened-progress bar, the playing flag and artwork, from local data only.
- One play order for CarPlay (both tabs and each show's list, the Shows tab), Siri's lists and auto-continue: episodes you are partway through first (newest play first, counting positions synced from the Mac and other devices), then not-started ones, oldest published first (ties keep the Larder order), then completed ones last, most recently completed first and marked "Played". An episode played to its end counts as completed before the Mac marks it. The phone's Larder list now uses the same order, so the phone, CarPlay, Siri and auto-continue always agree: the phone's sort menu and drag-to-reorder handles are gone (filter and search stay; the Mac keeps its own custom order and sort, and nothing in the shared Larder data changes). One shared rule, `InProgressOrdering` in WiltedLibrary.
- Auto-play next episode (Settings > Playback, on by default): when an episode finishes on the phone, the next one in the play order that is not completed starts from its saved position at the speed you were using, using audio already on the phone only; playback stops when none remain. A pause, a stop or a stale finish never advances.
- Episode artwork on the iPhone's Now Playing and in the CarPlay list, from a local artwork cache that is filled while the app is online and read from disk only (never fetched in the car or with the phone locked).
- Added Siri voice control of the iPhone player through App Intents (no entitlement or extension needed): play the next downloaded episode of a show, play a named episode, play the latest episode (of a show or overall) (spoken variants such as "tech crunch daily" match the feed title), pause, resume, skip forward or back, restart this episode, mark this episode completed (asks first, like the Larder button), what's playing, and list the episodes downloaded on the phone. Commands act only on audio already on the phone and never start a download; a spoken play never pauses an episode something else just started, and mark completed says "queued" rather than "marked completed" when the request could not be sent to the Mac yet; replies are one short sentence. Hosted tests run each intent against the real runtime, an on-disk library and the real audio engine, and check the exported App Intents metadata. The logic is framework-free in `WiltedLibrary` (`VoiceShowMatcher`, `VoiceCommandPlanner`) with unit tests; see `docs/siri-voice-commands.md`.
- Made the phone Larder populate on a real phone: the Mac now publishes an `available` media offer (revision, size, type, duration; no audio uploaded) for every prepared queued episode, and Get audio on the phone triggers the upload that flips it to `ready`. Once every requesting device has cached the audio the offer returns to `available` instead of vanishing, and offers for episodes that leave the Larder are withdrawn. An offer state an older reader does not know decodes as not ready.
- Added a `removeFromLarder` intent: the phone's Larder rows offer Remove from Larder (queue removal only, nothing deleted) instead of Skip, and the Mac applies it through the Larder row's own method with the usual once-only ledger, outcome and expiry.
- Published a compact transcript (timed cues, or plain text when the Mac has no timing, capped at 512 KB with truncation flagged) for a revision when the Mac answers that entry's media request, and withdrew it with the audio; it lives in the media zone as a named asset record, so no library fetch stages it and older readers never see it.
- Added the tracked transcript on the phone: after audio is verified the phone fetches the Mac's transcript for that revision (best effort; a missing or failed fetch never affects the audio) and caches it beside the audio, removed with it including Remove all in Settings, retried once per session when the detail opens. The episode detail and the full player (inline under its controls, no button) show a follow-along cue list (current cue bold with a leading marker, auto-scroll that pauses while you drag, tap a cue to seek the playing episode) or prose when the transcript has no timing.
- Published the Mac's lifetime statistics to the library as one named record the phone reads without a zone scan; the Mac republishes it on change through the existing publisher debounce.
- Made play position sync both ways: the Mac now adopts the phone's NowPlaying and Progress records as its own stored position (on sync start and every device-records poll) when the record is for the revision the Mac offers, is newer than the Mac's stored position and not below the entry's highest epoch, and the episode is neither finished nor playing, so an episode listened to on the phone starts at the phone's position when played on the Mac. A loaded, paused episode is moved without starting playback; a playing episode is never touched. A phone position that could not be published (offline, a failed write, a listen with no takeover) is now republished on the next sync instead of being lost. An idle checkpoint of an unchanged paused position keeps its original time, so backgrounding the Mac cannot make an old position look newer than the phone's.
- Added `LibraryRuntime.shared`, one process-wide library model, player and settings store that start without the iPhone window scene; the phone Larder now uses it, so CarPlay and Siri will share the same model, transport and audio session.
- Made the audio cache, transcripts and library snapshot explicitly readable while the phone is locked (`completeUntilFirstUserAuthentication`, the class CarPlay needs), (audio downloaded by earlier builds is repaired on the first listing), with a source audit that fails on any stronger class, `WhenUnlocked` keychain item or default-data-protection entitlement.
- Added the CarPlay audio entitlement (`com.apple.developer.carplay-audio`) to the iOS Development entitlements only and switched the Development configuration to manual signing with the "Wilted iOS Development" profile; the Production entitlements get the key when CarPlay is ready for every user.
- Added the framework-free car episode list model: only episodes already on the phone, in the Larder's order, capped to the car's list limit, with plain empty and loading statements that never ask the driver to use the phone.
- Made a launch with no signal list and play what is already downloaded: `LibraryRuntime.prepare()` loads the persisted library and the on-phone audio without any network call (`start()` adds the sync), so CarPlay and Siri do not wait on iCloud.
- Added the CarPlay audio scene: a `CPTemplateApplicationScene` (declared in the Info.plist scene manifest) with one list of the episodes on the phone and the system Now Playing template with a playback-rate button, driving the same shared player; it needs no iPhone window scene and never activates the audio session itself. Headless conformance tests (templates, depth, audio session, handlers, entitlement) plus `tests/test-carplay-config.sh`, and W-INV-015.
- Made offline resume work: the phone now keeps its own last position per episode on disk (readable while locked), so playing a downloaded episode with no signal resumes where the phone left off instead of at 0; a newer Mac position still wins by the handoff epoch rules. Seam tests cover one prepare across scenes, no audio session before play, and playback surviving the phone scene going to the background.
- Raised the iOS deployment target from 17.0 to 26.0 (app, tests, WiltedKit, Listener and CloudSync packages at `.iOS(.v26)`, swift-tools-version 6.2) and dropped the iOS 17 confirmation fallback in the Siri mark-completed intent; macOS targets are unchanged.
- Added in-app `INPlayMediaIntent` handling for spoken play requests: requests map onto the existing voice planner and play only downloaded episodes through the shared runtime. The Siri capability is in the App ID and the "Wilted iOS Development" profile, `com.apple.developer.siri` is in the Development entitlements (not Production), `NSSiriUsageDescription` is declared, and the phone asks for SiriKit authorization once. Whether the system routes the cell's request to the app (not an Intents extension) still needs a check in a car.
- Added two Siri commands (9 of 10 App Shortcut slots; one slot is free): "How much is left in Wilted" (time left on the loaded episode at the current speed, honest when nothing is loaded or the length is unknown), "Set speed to <0.75 to 2> in Wilted" (the player's six speeds; it sets the app's speed setting too, so it persists). The sleep timer (pause after 5 to 90 minutes or at the end of the episode, off cancels) is an intent for the Shortcuts app only, with no Siri phrase: on a device Siri handed every "sleep timer" request to the Clock app, which cannot end an episode. Added phrase synonyms to the existing shortcuts; none can collide with Siri's own pause and resume.
- Indexed downloaded episodes and their shows in Spotlight as App Intents indexed entities, kept in step with downloads and removals, and donated the play and mark-completed intents the person uses in the app so Siri can learn them (iOS 18 and earlier APIs only).
- Episodes now keep their own web page address from the feed's item link: it is read when a feed loads, stored in the library (store schema V15, migrated with the usual backup), and published in the library sync so the phone can offer it. Relative, non-web, credentialed or self-referential links are ignored.

### Changed
- Mac playlists: the Manual or Smart under a playlist's name is now a coloured tag (Smart in green, Manual in grey) so it no longer reads as part of the name, and the icons are Wilted's own basket (manual) and colander (smart). The playlist page shows the same tag, and New Playlist offers New Smart Playlist.
- The Mac pauses when its speakers or headphones go away (unplugged, disconnected, or the output device vanishes), saves the position and stays paused, as the iPhone does. Switching to another output that is still connected keeps playing.
- Preparation journals keep one row per heartbeat stage instead of one per tick, and a worker-unavailable or malformed-response error keeps its one-line detail.
- Removing an article writes its deleted flag and drops its playlist entry in one save; it no longer queues a sync tombstone, and removing an already removed article changes nothing.
- Mac episode listings now share Larder sorting and factual episode details across Feeds, playlists, History, and rule previews; playlists show Manual/Smart indicators, and Play uses the displayed ready-item order.
- Startup and existing player/review waits use the approved larder shelf serpentine at native 20/64-point sizes, with a 1.6-second indeterminate trace and a settled Reduce Motion frame. Startup recovery retains its actual counts in text. Feeds Refresh gains the approved circular arrow while active refresh keeps native progress and Cancel.
- Untrusted XML from followed feeds, articles, and dropped EPUBs now passes one linear bounded classifier before any parser exists, replacing the duplicate byte and regex scanners. Comments end at the first `-->` and processing instructions at the first `?>`, regardless of quotes; quotes hide `>` and `[` only inside tags and DOCTYPE literals; and a document's BOM or byte signature must agree with its XML declaration. UTF-8, UTF-16, UTF-32, ISO-8859-1, and windows-1252 are read; other, ambiguous, truncated, or contradictory encodings (including the UTF-7 shifted declaration the old scanners missed) are refused with a typed reason before parsing, and the original bytes still reach `XMLParser` unchanged. Podcast feeds refuse every DOCTYPE; article feeds and EPUB XML keep accepting a bare public/system DOCTYPE; entity declarations and internal subsets stay refused everywhere; both no-resolution controls are set and existing consumer-specific resolver behavior is retained; the canary checks do not trace resource I/O.
- Preparation progress now updates the journal row by exact event ID; library publication on download-byte updates skips full transcript and preparation-history reads.
- An unresolved ad review retains the proven original playable audio with a durable warning and truthful request, failure, and unresolved-segment counts; genuine audio, render, and proof errors remain fatal.
- Mac audio commands no longer wait for older transcript or preparation-cut presentation; stale reads cannot replace the loaded selection, and Pause stays immediate (timings only dispatch-to-audio-call, never audible proof).
- Podcast Remove and Mark completed share a reversible finish that retains downloaded audio and measured progress. Undo restores prior queue placement and decision provenance without playing for 10 seconds, holding the freed automatic slot until the window is durably sealed. Restart recovers waiting admissions, and a later explicit Restore may exceed the kept limit without evicting another episode; automatic Keep waits until capacity returns. Phone Restore reuses verified retained audio offline.
- V20 reversible confirmed same-feed association keeps all raw identities, media, checkpoints, and queue slots, folds display, and expands logical actions and capacity; original phone timestamps and newest choice guards are preserved, preferring exact-source handoff first and alternate only with matching prepared hash, bytes, media type, revisions, and duration within 0.05 seconds, without destructive merge or live association application.
- Mac Status view places active work after actionable rows and displays live observed status in active metadata space (up to two lines for stage detail); it removes the redundant empty-state article reminder while keeping toolbar Add and File New actions intact.
- Feed names can be edited inline: Return saves, Escape cancels, and an empty name restores the publisher title. Refresh preserves a saved name; Unsubscribe retains its confirmation.
- Larder counts belong to the lifecycle filters and global Download/Prepare actions; groups retain Play first and Remove without repeating download/preparation controls.
- Natural podcast completion chooses the first displayed eligible Ready row, including earlier unfinished episodes. Playlist and audiobook completion keep their own rules.
- Alphabetical sorting on Mac and iPhone ignores a leading “The” case-insensitively without changing displayed titles.
- Share keeps its normal control when a page address is unavailable and sends `title - show` as text instead.
- Subscribing on the Mac no longer asks how many episodes to list; new feeds use the Episodes listed default in Settings.
- A pasted feed with no audio episodes is offered as an article feed to follow instead of being refused; a feed with neither audio nor posts says "This feed has no audio and no posts to follow."
- The Mac Larder is one list grouped Ready, Active work, Downloaded, then Not downloaded, with articles and books in the same list and a kind filter (All, Podcasts, Articles, Audiobooks); the iPhone Larder has the same kind filter.
- Mac startup and work recovery show counted progress for each step instead of a single waiting message.
- The Mac Now Playing pane's episode title is smaller, and the narrow sidebar rail shows the Ready audio time under a play icon.
- iPhone Larder: rows grouped by feed no longer repeat the feed name, Sort and Group are titled sections ("None" is now "No Grouping"), Sort and Filter show a filled icon while active, and episode detail puts Share below Play and Download.
- iPhone Settings: the device ID moves into a Diagnostics disclosure, sync detail appears inline only when sync has an error, and the listening totals are titled "This iPhone".
- Records equal audio: finishing an episode, or skipping it from Feeds, or retiring it, now deletes its downloaded audio, and unsubscribing deletes the feed's audio, once the change is saved (a file another episode still uses stays); a manual Mark completed is the exception, keeping its audio, transcript and revisions so Undo restores it. A restored episode comes back as not downloaded; Feeds keeps the history so it can be downloaded again. The unsubscribe confirmation says so.
- Re-downloading an episode deletes the copy it replaces, and the Mac startup has a "Reclaiming storage" step that deletes audio no record names and clears records whose audio is missing.
- Mac playlist create, rename, delete, add, remove, reorder and rules edits appear immediately while their write runs and roll back only the playlist that failed; Add to Playlist, the playlist's title actions and a labelled Share options panel stay visible.
- Per-feed match rules match case-insensitively, and their preview names the rule that decided each episode.
- The Mac UI receipt check moved from the pre-push hook to `make install`: a push now runs `make validate` only, while `make install` refuses unless a green `make native-ui` receipt covers the Mac UI surface of the tree being installed (`WILTED_SKIP_UI_RECEIPT=1` installs anyway with a warning naming the receipt it ignores). `WILTED_GATE_LEGS=<legs>` reruns single legs of the native gate; such a filtered run is reported as `filtered=<n>` and can never record a receipt.
- First launch with schema V20 upgrades older Mac libraries for confirmed same-feed episode associations. Migration checkpoints the source, retains a verified backup, rehearses migration on a disposable copy, and verifies row counts before opening. If migration or verification fails, it restores the untouched retained backup; backups are never deleted automatically. A store newer than the running build is refused untouched.
- Removing an episode or unsubscribing on the Mac now asks first. Unsubscribe "Removes this subscription, its episodes and their saved positions. Downloaded audio files stay on disk."
- `make install` now builds the Development configuration with live iCloud, and the library publisher is the default sync engine (`WILTED_LIBRARY_SYNC=0` keeps the legacy path, `=1` forces the publisher).
- Library sync is bound to the iCloud account it started with. Signing out, switching accounts, or an account that cannot be confirmed stops sending and asks for review; pending changes stay with their original account until review resumes them.
- iPhone Settings reports "Fetched" with the time of this phone's last successful library read, instead of claiming "Up to date".
- The Mac player stays in the same position across Larder, Feeds and Settings at a given window width, and preserves its selected section across navigation and resizing. Feed setup now shows 5, 10 or Custom episodes to list, with a numeric field only for Custom.
- Mac Larder episode titles open the same linked, scrollable show notes as Feeds, without changing playback or the queue. Larder sorting now offers Length, Age, Alphabetical and Custom, with a saved ascending/descending direction for ordered modes and unknown lengths or publication dates last.
- The Mac Feeds page has one heading and one place for its operation message and Undo. It lists active operations first, then New episodes, Subscriptions and Off the list. Skip uses an outlined style beside the filled Keep, and a relative "Last refreshed" time shows on Feeds and, after a refresh, on the Larder.
- Larder episode rows on Mac and iOS now display a single second metadata line formatted as "Feed - duration - date" using the feed title, total source duration, and publication date; the iPhone keeps New or remaining-time status on a separate line, with completion and lifecycle feedback distinct.
- Each device now has one 30 s sync tick and everything periodic batches into it. The Mac's round is one batched read (intents and the phone's playback records in a single request) followed by its own writes; the phone's round runs only while the app is in front or playing and reads the playback records and the Mac's offers in one request, the library state every 10th round, and the Mac's answer to a pending decision in the same read. The 5 s decision poll, the 2 s offer poll, the separate handoff observe loop, the confirming fetch after a takeover, the Mac's per-change retry timers and the statistics write on every listening-clock change are gone. A user action still sends its one write at once; confirmation waits for the next round. A rate limit now holds the whole tick for the server's Retry-After, and the Mac no longer refetches its own intent index record while it is known to be missing. Idle, the Mac made about 17 CloudKit requests a minute (measured over 40 minutes); modeled by tests it now makes about 2 to 2.5 and the phone about 2.3, still a little over the 1 to 2 a minute goal, since one read per 30 s round is already 2 a minute (to be measured on the installed builds); see `docs/2026-10-01-sync-cadence.md`.
- Pull to refresh (Larder and Settings) runs a full round at once and restarts the 30 s timer; while iCloud is rate limiting it sends nothing and the retry banner shows, and pulls made during a round share it.
- The Mac Larder count line reads "Ready: N episodes", the one term the iPhone uses, instead of "Waiting for you"; it counts the Ready group, the same figure as the sidebar's Ready row, not every episode in the Larder.
- One shared playback speed list (`PlaybackSpeeds` in WiltedKit): 0.5 to 2.0 in 0.25 steps, used by the Mac speed menus and media keys, the iPhone default-speed setting, Now Playing, CarPlay and the Siri speed intent. The Mac gains 1.75 and the iPhone and Siri gain 0.5; a stored value off the step shows on the nearest one.
- The Siri "play latest" shortcut is now "Play something in Wilted" (also "Play an episode of <show> in Wilted", "Play the first episode of <show> in Wilted", "Play my top Wilted episode"): it plays the first downloaded episode in the app's play order (partway through first, then not-started oldest first), like the phone list and auto-continue, not the newest published. The car's spoken "newest" request still plays the newest published.
- Removed the "Skip forward in Wilted" and "Skip back in Wilted" Siri phrases: plain "skip" and the car's skip buttons already do it. The skip intents stay in the Shortcuts app.
- Rebuilt the iPhone Larder as one list of episodes the Mac has prepared (a ready or available media offer, or audio already on the phone), with sort (custom, newest, oldest, shortest, show, title; remembered), an All / On phone / Available filter, search over title, show and notes, artwork thumbnails, publication dates, and Play that resumes from the Mac's last position. New and Removed sections and the Keep and Restore actions are gone from the phone. The rows now use Wilted type and a faint lettuce watermark, with SF Symbol controls: download or delete the local copy (delete asks first), play or pause, and Mark completed (asks first, and completes the episode on the Mac too). Remove from Larder is no longer offered on the phone, and reorder handles show only in Custom order.

### Removed
- The Apple Podcasts library importer (`wilted-podcast-import`). Importing from other podcast apps is a possible future feature.
- The unused iPhone platform-contract reducers, the CloudKit transport spike app, and the old credential-free probes (speech IPC, signed speech runtime, article extraction, persistence and audio contract) with their gate legs. The article extraction fixtures moved to `Producer/Tests/Fixtures/ArticleExtraction`.
- The unused nominated-pod share bound in ad detection and the ad corpus bracketing measurement that only existed to calibrate it.
- The legacy article sync engine in every project: the Mac `WiltedMacSyncLifecycle` and its publication queue, the `WiltedSync` and `WiltedSyncTesting` packages, the Producer's outbound sync queue (tombstones, playback sidecars, sync status, snapshots), CloudSync's article record transport and mapper, and the frozen record contract in `contracts/cloudkit` with its gate leg. Library sync is the only sync path; `WILTED_LIBRARY_SYNC=0` turns it off. The audio publication budget moved to `contracts/audio/publication-budget.json`. Library schema V21 deletes the leftover legacy sync rows (tombstones and repository state) and the unused sync columns on first launch, after the usual verified backup.
- Four transcript-cleanup worker modules that only tests imported, and the hand-copied preparation source-hash pins.
- The Batch 1 HTML prototype and its Playwright test leg are gone; UX is now reviewed in the native app.
